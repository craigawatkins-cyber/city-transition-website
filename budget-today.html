Where the money goes today

Right now, two separate governments run Holiday Island side by side: the City, and HISID. Add them together, and this small lake town already spends more than most Arkansas cities its size.

Why we run higher — three honest reasons

We're spread out

Holiday Island maintains 71 miles of roads. A typical city our size has about 10. More road miles means more paving, more ditches, more snow removal, more of everything, spread across a much larger footprint per resident.

We're an older community

Most residents are retired or of retirement age, which means a higher share of medical and EMS calls, and a harder time recruiting volunteer firefighters — both of which push public safety costs up.

We invest in recreation

Two golf courses, a marina, pools, trails, and a community center are a big part of why people choose to live here — and a big part of the budget. It's a deliberate above-average investment, not an accident.
