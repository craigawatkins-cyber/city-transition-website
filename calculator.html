What will this cost me?

Every property is different. Use the calculator below to estimate your own annual cost change under the proposed transition, using the actual formulas from the Mayor's feasibility study.

Before you start: these are preliminary estimates from the May and July 2026 feasibility study presentations — not adopted city rates or final figures. Actual costs depend on decisions still to be made by petitions, a sales-tax election, and the City Council. Use this as a planning estimate, not a bill.
