About this site

Holiday Island's Future is an independent, resident-built project. It is not an official publication of the City of Holiday Island, HISID, or any government body.

Why this site exists

In 2026, Mayor Kees presented a feasibility study exploring whether the City of Holiday Island should take over all services currently provided by the Holiday Island Suburban Improvement District (HISID). It's a complex, numbers-heavy topic spread across two long presentations — easy for residents to miss, and hard to fully absorb in a single public meeting. This site exists to make that same information easier to find, easier to read, and easier to apply to your own household, while making an honest case for why the author believes this transition is worth pursuing.

Sources

Figures on this site come from two kinds of sources. It matters which is which: the feasibility-study presentations below are proposal figures — not adopted rates. The Arkansas-law sources further down explain how state tax rules work in general, independent of this specific proposal.

Holiday Island feasibility study (proposal figures)

  • "Can the City Do It All?" — A Feasibility Study, presented by Mayor Kees on May 2, 2026 (updated May 6, 2026).
  • "The Future Holiday Island — Session 2: Protecting Our Citizens," presented by Mayor Kees on July 11, 2026 (revised July 13, 2026).

Both presentations were delivered in public forums. The original slide decks are referenced by the city itself at cityofholidayisland.com, under About/Contact → Services.

How figures were checked

Every dollar figure, statistic, and formula on this site was transcribed directly from those two presentations. Where a table in the original slides could not be reconstructed with full confidence (for example, due to how numbers were laid out on the slide), this site either omits that specific figure or notes the uncertainty rather than guessing. Where the two presentations gave slightly different estimates for the same figure (for instance, the Public Safety Urban Service District fee was refined between May and July), this site uses the more recent figure for live calculations and preserves the original as a labeled reference.

A few sections are the deliberate exception: the Growth Plan and Tax Comparison pages extend beyond the study with the site author's own analysis and independently researched figures (sales tax and property tax rates for nearby towns and regional cities, pulled from public rate-lookup services and property-tax data aggregators). The fixed-income note on the Cost Calculator page does the same with Arkansas's homestead property tax relief programs, sourced from the state Department of Finance and Administration. The short-term rental note on the Funding page does the same with City of Holiday Island ordinances and Arkansas's Advertising & Promotion Commission statute. All are clearly labeled as such where they appear, with their own source lists.

A note on point of view

This site's author is a Holiday Island resident who supports moving forward with this transition, and most pages here make that case directly. The one deliberate exception is the Public Safety page, covering the still-undecided choice between a sheriff contract and a new city police department — a genuine open question the source study itself leaves to a resident petition, presented here as neutrally as we can manage.

Questions or corrections

If you believe a figure on this site is inaccurate or out of date, we want to know. Please raise it at a City Council or HISID Board of Commissioners meeting, or reach out through the City's official contact channels at cityofholidayisland.com.
